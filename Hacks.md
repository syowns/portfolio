<h2 id="understanding-_configyml">Understanding <code class="language-plaintext highlighter-rouge">_config.yml</code></h2>

<p>The <code class="language-plaintext highlighter-rouge">_config.yml</code> file is the heart of your GitHub Pages site configuration. It controls site-wide settings such as the title, description, theme, navigation, and more.</p>

<p><strong>Explore and Try:</strong></p>

<ul>
  <li>Open your project’s <code class="language-plaintext highlighter-rouge">_config.yml</code> file.</li>
  <li>Find and edit the following settings:
    <ul>
      <li><code class="language-plaintext highlighter-rouge">title</code>: The name of your site (appears in the browser tab and header).</li>
      <li><code class="language-plaintext highlighter-rouge">description</code>: A short summary of your site’s purpose.</li>
      <li><code class="language-plaintext highlighter-rouge">theme</code>: The Jekyll theme your site uses.</li>
      <li><code class="language-plaintext highlighter-rouge">url</code> and <code class="language-plaintext highlighter-rouge">baseurl</code>: Used for correct link generation.</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
  <li>Add a new custom variable (for example, <code class="language-plaintext highlighter-rouge">author: Your Name</code>) and display it in your layout or a page using ``.</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Challenge:</strong></p>

<ul>
  <li>Change the site title and description, then run your site locally to see the changes.</li>
  <li>Try adding a new navigation link or menu item by editing <code class="language-plaintext highlighter-rouge">_config.yml</code>.</li>
  <li>Research other options you can set in <code class="language-plaintext highlighter-rouge">_config.yml</code> to customize your site.</li>
</ul>

<blockquote>
  <p>Understanding and customizing <code class="language-plaintext highlighter-rouge">_config.yml</code> is key to making your GitHub Pages site truly your own!</p>
</blockquote>
